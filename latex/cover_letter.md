Dear Editors,

Please consider our manuscript, "Anxiety symptoms and coping strategies among high school students in {Vietnam} after {COVID-19} pandemic: A mixed-method evaluation". It combines four PISA questionnaire cohorts with 2022 school-policy data and public context indicators. The focus is measurement-tested mathematics anxiety, item-response patterns and within-cycle educational associations. Belonging fails scalar and targeted partial-scalar invariance; the article does not claim a validated latent decline. DFL is presented as overlap-limited sensitivity, and IPW/SHAP are not interpreted as causal designs. OECD's warning on Vietnamese performance comparability precludes an achievement trend claim.
